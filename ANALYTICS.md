# Website analytics

This portfolio can use GoatCounter for lightweight, cookie-free visitor
statistics. GoatCounter's hosted service is free for reasonable public usage,
including personal websites.

## View visitor statistics

Tracking is configured for the `stephen-account` GoatCounter site. View the
statistics in the [GoatCounter dashboard](https://stephen-account.goatcounter.com/).

The tracker counts the initial page load and Astro client-side page changes.
GoatCounter provides aggregate visitor and page-view statistics; it does not
identify visitors by name. Browser extensions may block analytics requests, and
your own visits can be excluded in GoatCounter's settings.
